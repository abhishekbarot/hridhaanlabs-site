Arcade · Ages 4+

Hridhaan's Space Rocket

Blast off, catch falling stars and steer clear of tumbling space rocks. The game speeds up every 10 stars, so there's always a new best score to chase.

Play in your browser 🚀 Google Play: coming soon

What's in the game

⭐Catch starsEvery star is a point. Ten stars takes you up a level.
💎Diamond starsBlue stars worth 5 points. They fall slowly, so you can catch them.
🪨Dodge rocksEach hit costs a heart. From level 4 some rocks wiggle sideways.
🌈Super shieldCatch the rainbow star and rocks just go BOOM for 8 seconds.
💖Extra livesFrom level 5, catch hearts to get up to five lives.
🚀Unlock rocketsStars add up across games to unlock the Ocean, Gold and Rainbow rockets.
  • No ads
  • No in-app purchases
  • No data collected
  • Works offline

How to play

Drag your finger anywhere in space and the rocket follows, or tap the big arrow buttons. On a computer, use the arrow keys. Press pause any time.
