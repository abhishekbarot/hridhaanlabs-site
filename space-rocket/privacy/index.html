Privacy Policy

Hridhaan's Space Rocket · Android app ID com.hridhaan.spacerocket · Effective 26 October 2026

Hridhaan's Space Rocket is a game for children, published by Hridhaan Labs. We built it so that it collects nothing about the people who play it.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, send, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no way to enter a name, email, or photo.

What stays on the device

The game saves four small things on the device so it can remember them between games: the best score, the total stars collected, which rocket is selected, and whether sound is on. In the Android app they are kept in the app's own storage; in the browser version, in the browser's local storage. They never leave the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the browser's site data, deletes them.

Ads, tracking and third parties

  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking tools.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No links that take children out of the Android app.

Permissions

The Android app asks for no special permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.

This website

hridhaanlabs.in uses no cookies and no analytics. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of visit) for security and reliability. We do not use them to identify anyone.

Children's privacy

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children under 13. This is consistent with Google Play's Families policy.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
